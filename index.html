About

Independent research by Tirth Parekh.

I’m Tirth Parekh, a Business Systems Analyst with a background in Business Analytics. I created Bullnexa as a personal platform to document how I research public companies, sectors, market trends, and potential investment opportunities.

My research process looks at business fundamentals, earnings trends, valuation, industry context, price action, catalysts, and downside risk. I’m especially interested in financials, infrastructure, energy transition, solar, industrials, healthcare, and emerging growth themes across Indian and global equity markets.

Bullnexa is about building a disciplined research process, not predicting every market move. Every idea should have a thesis, supporting evidence, key risks, and conditions that could prove the thesis wrong.
