About

Independent by design.
Disciplined by process.

I’m Tirth Parekh, and I created Bullnexa to turn market ideas into structured, evidence-based research. My background in Business Analytics and systems analysis shapes how I look at companies: understand the business, identify the key drivers, test the thesis, and make the risks visible.

My research combines business fundamentals, earnings trends, valuation, industry dynamics, catalysts, and price context. I’m especially interested in financials, infrastructure, energy transition, industrials, healthcare, and emerging growth opportunities across Indian and global equity markets.

Bullnexa is not about predicting every market move or chasing stock tips. Each research idea should have a clear thesis, supporting evidence, key risks, and specific conditions that could change the view. The goal is simple: make better-informed investment research decisions through a disciplined process.

Principles
01

Research first

Every research idea should begin with facts, data, and a clear investment thesis.

02

Show the risk

Strong research looks at what can go wrong, not only what could go right.

03

Respect uncertainty

Markets are unpredictable. Research improves decisions, but never guarantees outcomes.

04

Stay transparent

Separate facts from opinions and clearly disclose positions, assumptions, and potential conflicts.
